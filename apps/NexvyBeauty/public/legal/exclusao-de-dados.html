Exclusão de dados — Instagram / NexvyBeauty

User data deletion instructions for Meta App Review (NEXVY - IGLOG / Instagram Login).

How to request deletion

  1. In the salon app, open Conexões and disconnect Instagram Comercial, or
  2. Email dpo@nexvy.tech from the same account used in the product. Subject: “Exclusão de dados Instagram”.

What we delete

  • Instagram Login access token (encrypted at rest)
  • Instagram user id, username, and connection metadata
  • Direct messages stored in the salon inbox for that Instagram connection, when the controller (the salon) or the data subject requests it

What we keep when the law requires it

Billing records and consent logs may be retained for legal/tax periods (LGPD art. 16). We confirm completion at dpo@nexvy.tech.

Como solicitar (PT-BR)

Desconecte o Instagram em Conexões no app do salão ou escreva para dpo@nexvy.tech. Apagamos token, id da conta e mensagens Direct ligadas à conexão, ressalvado prazo legal.
